AIditing

AIditing is a private, single-user video editing tool. It takes raw interview footage, transcribes it, and assembles a short vertical cut from the parts worth keeping.

Once a cut is approved, AIditing uploads it to its owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. It is operated by one person, on one machine, for one account — there is no sign-up, no hosted service, and no other users.

The tool runs locally. Footage, transcripts and rendered videos stay on the owner's computer, and the only thing ever sent to TikTok is a video the owner has explicitly chosen to publish.
